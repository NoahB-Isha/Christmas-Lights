Group props

The pool on the left holds every prop not yet in a group, sorted by theme. Drag a prop onto the empty board to spawn a group, then drag more props into it (shift-click / ⌘A picks several at once, or drag a whole theme by its ⇥ button). Drag a prop back to the pool to remove it from its group. Moving a prop clears its controller/port — re-run auto-assign in Studio → Props & groups. Double-click a card to see it big.
