# Site plan — P4 Pavilion drive-through course (received 2026-09-19)

Source: `PLAN-2026.06.26_P4 PAV site proposal v5 TOPO_AVK Received-Model.pdf` (Isha Foundation, drawn by jh,
rev 5, nominal scale 1 in = 100 ft, north up). Rendered at 150 dpi as `assets/site/site_plan_2026.png`; the true scale of
that render, measured from the drawing's own scale bar (400 ft = 1027 px), is **4.674 in per pixel**.

**Status (owner, 2026-09-19): this is LAST YEAR's layout, for orientation only. Nothing is set. The DMX props still
need to be grouped and placed. The zone names (Kids, Animals, Deer, Stars…) are to be disregarded for now.**

## What is NOT ours

Christian area, Manger scene, Tunnel, Exit, Pavilion and Selfie area all have their own **non-DMX** lighting.
The DMX pixel-prop show is separate from those.

## Route, in driving order (one-way loop, enters SE, exits NW)

1. WELCOME gate (SE corner) — props both sides of the road; elec transformer + wifi panel + light pole.
2. Straight stretch heading NW (labelled Kids / Animals / Deer last year) — props both sides.
3. Curve N/NE (labelled Stars) — long arc of props on the right (east) side. Movie screen with tiered seating off to the east.
4. Junction island: Pavilion/Concession, Selfie area photo stations, the 125 ft tree (90 ft diameter). Transformer NE of the island. (non-DMX)
5. Christian area / Manger scene heading W/NW (non-DMX), then the Tunnel (non-DMX), then Exit (non-DMX).

## Infrastructure on the plan

- Elec transformers: Welcome gate, junction east of the Stars arc, NE of the tree island, Christian area.
- Elec meters: junction near the Pavilion, Christian area. Wifi panels: gate, junction, Christian area (2), Manger.
- Light poles: Welcome, Stars arc, Christian area, Exit, plus two east of the course.

## Implications for the DMX show

- The DMX props most plausibly live along the entry straight and the Stars arc (the stretches with prop rows both
  sides and power at both ends) — to be decided with the owner. Hardware team's working figure: ~300 ft, 6 clusters,
  40 controllers.
- Cars drive SE→NW: course-wide effects should run in driving order.
